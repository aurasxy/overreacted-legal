Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overreacted ("we", "us") publishes short-form story videos on our own social media accounts (@overreactedhq on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube). To do that, we use a small private publishing tool (the "Overreacted Publisher" app) that connects to those platforms through their official APIs. This policy explains what that tool accesses and how it is handled.

Who the app is for

The Overreacted Publisher is an internal tool. It is used only to upload videos to accounts that we own and operate. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access, collect or post anything to anyone else's account.

Information we access

When we connect one of our own accounts, the platform shares, with our explicit permission:

  • Basic account information: the account's platform ID and display name, so the tool knows which of our accounts it is posting to.
  • Access tokens: credentials issued by the platform that allow the tool to upload videos to that account.
  • Upload results: the IDs and status of the videos we publish.

We do not collect information about viewers, followers, commenters or any other users. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

How we use it

Solely to upload and publish our own original videos to our own accounts, and to keep a record of what was posted.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on our own computer and are never shared. Nothing is sold, rented or given to third parties, and nothing is used for advertising.

Platform policies

Our use of each platform's API follows that platform's terms. For YouTube, this tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings, or Instagram's / Facebook's "Apps and websites" settings.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected and are deleted when we disconnect it. See Data deletion for how to request removal of any data.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly process any child's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: send a direct message to @overreactedhq on TikTok or Instagram.
