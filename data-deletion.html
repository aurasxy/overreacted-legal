Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The Overreacted Publisher only connects to accounts owned by Overreacted, and stores nothing about anyone else. If you connected an account to it and want all related data removed, you can do either of the following:

Option 1: Revoke access yourself (instant)

  • Facebook / Instagram: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → select "Overreacted Publisher" → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps → "Overreacted Publisher" → Remove access.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → "Overreacted Publisher" → Remove access.

Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted by the app the next time it checks the account.

Option 2: Ask us

Send a direct message to @overreactedhq on TikTok or Instagram with the subject "Data deletion". We delete the account's stored token and any related records within 30 days and confirm by reply.
